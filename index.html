    What's modelled vs. what would need more data

    • Modelled directly from your workbook — per-SKU margin/profit (original and latest purchase price), company & size ranking, fast/moderate/slow/dead-stock classification, purchase quantity + priority, size↔brand matrix, and full vehicle-model fitment (parsed from the Vehicle Model / Models Applicable column).
    • Not in this workbook — branch/zone MSDC targets, live current stock, supplier lead times. Upload a workbook that includes those and this build won't parse them; ask me to extend the dashboard if you need that tab back.
    • Trailing period — your sheet currently has sales logged for — month(s) (columns for the rest of the year are still blank). All "avg monthly" and movement-class figures are averaged over that many months, and update automatically as you fill in more months and re-upload.
